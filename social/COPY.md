# Rushly × Partner — Social Copy

Swap-in copy for the templates. Each post in `social/` ships with **Angle 1**
(partnership announcement). To change angle, replace the `.tag`, `.headline`,
`.subcopy`, and `.chips` in the HTML with one of the blocks below. The word in
`<em>…</em>` renders as the Playfair italic tan accent.

Partner shown: **Avenue Bakery** (Avenue Bakery on Fourth, Salt Lake City).
To use a different partner, swap the name everywhere and update the location line.
