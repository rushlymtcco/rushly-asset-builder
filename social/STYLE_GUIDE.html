03 — Co-brand lockup

Rushly × Partner.

Every partner post leads with the same lockup: the Rushly mark, a tan multiply sign, and the partner's name in Playfair italic. Equal weight, equal respect.

× Avenue Bakery

✓ Do

Use the tan wordmark (rushly-logo_alt3) on navy, and the navy logo on cream. Keep clear space around the lockup equal to the height of the box icon.

✓ Do

Set the partner name in Playfair italic, cream or white. The × is Montserrat, tan, ~70% opacity — a connector, not a focal point.

✕ Don't

Don't stretch, recolor, add shadows to, or rotate the Rushly mark. Don't place the navy logo on a navy/photo background where it disappears.

✕ Don't

Don't make the partner larger than Rushly (or vice-versa), and never swap the accent color away from tan.

10 — Voice & captions

Talk like a helpful friend.

Warm, reassuring, and practical — you're helping a missionary mom send love across the miles. Confident, never salesy.

Do

  • Lead with the benefit: same-day, easy, local.
  • Speak to the parent / sender, warmly.
  • Be specific — name the location and cutoff time.
  • One clear CTA: order at rushlymtc.com.
  • A tasteful emoji or two (📦 💛 ☕).

Don't

  • Hype or pressure ("ACT NOW!!!").
  • Corporate jargon or logistics-speak.
  • Emoji walls or ALL CAPS sentences.
  • Bury the offer under three paragraphs.
  • Forget the partner shout-out / tag.
